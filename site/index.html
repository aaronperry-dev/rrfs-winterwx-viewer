Data: NOAA/NCEP RRFS v1 ensemble members and REFS mean from the NOAA Open Data (AWS S3) bucket, fetched field by field with byte-range requests. The member mean, spread, minimum, maximum, percentiles and probabilities are computed in your browser from the members you leave switched on; the REFS mean is NOAA's own (it also blends time-lagged members and the HRRR). Fields that need pressure-level profiles (Kuchera and max-T-aloft snowfall, DGZ metrics, warm nose, gusts, blizzard) exist only for the members.
